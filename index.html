About Me

Counselor & Speaker.

I grew up in the Bay Area and graduated from a public high school of 650+ students. I was 1 of 10 in my class to be admitted to a Top 10 university. That number stuck with me. Not because of prestige, but because it represented how often talented students slip through the cracks of the college admissions system.

“Today, I help students close that gap. I fundamentally believe that every student, with the right strategy, can get into a top university.”

With 6Million+ views across platforms, I mentor students from every background and at every stage of the college application journey: from freshmen just beginning to dream, to seniors fine-tuning their final essays. I help each student uncover their unique story and coach them to tell their story in a memorable way.

On the Stage

Beyond 1:1 coaching, I am a speaker at education conferences, summits, and events.

My topics include narratives from my own journey, college admissions strategy, authentic storytelling, and personal brand building for teens and young adults.

Service 01 · Strategy Call

Private Call with Eileen

My favorite hour of the week is jumping on Zoom for a private 1-on-1 with a student who’s ready to get serious about their college applications.

What we’ll cover

  • College application strategy and how to position yourself
  • Essay brainstorming and revision
  • Resume and extracurricular/activity enhancement
  • Career and personal-brand building
  • Anything from high school planning through life after college

How it works

One 60-minute Zoom session, fully tailored to where you are right now. By the end, you’ll know exactly what your strongest, most competitive application looks like — and the steps to get there.

You’ll also walk away with the personal application materials that helped me get into Duke with $150k+ in merit scholarships.

Track record

My students have been admitted to Harvard, Princeton, Stanford, MIT, and other top universities — most of them after just a single private call.
